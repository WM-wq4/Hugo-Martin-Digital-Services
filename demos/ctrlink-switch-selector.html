CTRLink Switch Family Selector — independent prototype
Requirements-based product navigation

Which CTRLink switch family fits the application?

A proof-of-concept that converts published CTRLink product information into a faster first-pass selection workflow. It narrows families by management, PoE, fiber, port-count range and environmental needs.

Prototype only. Built independently from public Contemporary Controls product pages. It is not an official Contemporary Controls tool and does not replace application engineering or final specification review.
