Industrial PoE Switch Selector

A proof-of-concept that converts CTC Union's 2026 Product Selection Guide into a requirements-based selector. Set objective requirements and see only standard industrial PoE switches that satisfy every selected constraint.

27 standard industrial PoE models in verified demo dataset
No model in this verified demo dataset satisfies every selected constraint.
Prototype scope: The dataset covers the managed and unmanaged Industrial PoE Switch tables in CTC Union's 2026 Product Selection Guide (printed pages 25–30). It intentionally excludes separate cyber-hardened, EN50155, IEC 61850-3 and rackmount families. This tool filters published specifications; it does not make network-design recommendations.
Source: CTC Union 2026 Product Selection Guide v1.9. Specifications should be rechecked with CTC Union before purchase or deployment.
